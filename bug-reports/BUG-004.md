**# BUG-005 — Building Is Placed When Selecting Another Building**

****Severity:**** High

****Status:**** Open

**### What happened**

When the player selects a building but does not have enough wood to place it, the building is correctly not placed.

However, when the player then clicks the icon of another building, the previously selected building is placed automatically.

**### Steps to Reproduce**

1. Select a building that requires more wood than the player currently has.

2. Try to place it on the map.

3. The building is not placed because there is not enough wood.

4. Click the icon of another building in the building menu.

**### Expected Result**

The player should switch to the newly selected building without placing the previous building.

**### Actual Result**

The previously selected building is placed automatically when the player clicks the icon of another building.

**### Status**

Open. The bug has been identified and still needs to be fixed.
