### Bug #5 – Building can be placed without enough wood

**Description:**
The player can place a building even when they don't have enough wood to build it.

**Steps to reproduce:**

1. Open the building menu.
2. Select the House, which costs 50 wood.
3. Make sure the player has less than 50 wood.
4. Choose a location on the map.
5. Click to place the building.

**Expected result:**
The building should not be placed if the player doesn't have enough wood.

**Actual result:**
The building is placed even though the player doesn't have enough wood.

**Priority:** High
**Severity:** High
