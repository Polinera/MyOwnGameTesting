# BUG-002 — Building Appeared Above the Ground

**Severity:** Medium
**Status:** Fixed

### What happened

After placing a building, it sometimes appeared too high above the ground instead of standing correctly on the map.

### Steps to Reproduce

1. Open the building menu.
2. Select the house.
3. Move the building preview to a suitable location.
4. Place the house.
5. Check its position relative to the ground.

### Expected Result

The building is placed at the correct height and stands on the ground.

### Actual Result

The building appeared too high above the ground and looked like it was floating.

### Status

Fixed. The building placement position was corrected so that the building is placed at the proper height.
